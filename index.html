Plantoria Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Draft prepared for the upcoming advertising-enabled release

Plantoria is a local-first Android plant-care application. This Privacy Policy explains what information Plantoria stores on your device, what information may be sent to third-party services, and the choices available to you.

1. Data stored locally

Plant profiles, care history, observations, problems, reminders, growing conditions, recommendations, experiments, and private plant photos are primarily stored locally on your device. Plantoria does not require an account for its core features.

2. Product analytics

Plantoria uses Google Analytics for Firebase only after you explicitly opt in to product analytics. Plantoria sends coarse product-usage events such as feature usage, plant-count ranges, and session-duration ranges. Plantoria does not intentionally include plant names, notes, photos, free-text symptoms, or exact locations in its custom analytics events.

Analytics collection is disabled by default in the application configuration and is enabled only after your consent. You can disable product analytics again from Plantoria's Privacy settings. Collection of the Android Advertising ID by Firebase Analytics is disabled by Plantoria.

3. Advertising

The upcoming advertising-enabled version of Plantoria uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's advertising systems, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), interactions with ads and the app, diagnostic information, and device or app identifiers for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention purposes.

Where required, Plantoria uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent before requesting ads. Available privacy choices depend on your region and applicable law. Plantoria does not sell your locally stored plant records, notes, or photos to advertisers.

4. Online requests initiated by app features

  • Wikidata: a plant-species search term is sent when you explicitly start an online species search.
  • Weather provider: a place name entered by you may be sent when weather is requested for relevant outdoor, balcony, or greenhouse context.

Plantoria does not request precise or background device location for these features.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: online species search, weather, analytics (if enabled), consent management, and advertising.
  • Notifications: care reminders on supported Android versions.
  • Receive boot completed: restoring scheduled reminders after reboot, app update, or relevant time changes.

6. Backups and exports

Android automatic application backup is disabled. User-controlled ZIP backup and JSON export use Android's system file picker. Restore is initiated explicitly by the user.

7. Third-party services

Plantoria may interact with Google Firebase / Google Analytics, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and UMP, Wikidata, and a weather-data provider. These services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Plant-data provenance and source licences are also shown inside Plantoria under Sources & Licenses.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored Plantoria data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to any backups you created yourself. Analytics and advertising information processed by Google is retained according to the applicable Google service settings, policies, and your consent choices.

9. Your choices

You can decline or later disable Plantoria product analytics. Where applicable, advertising privacy and consent choices are provided through Google's consent interface and can be revisited from the app's privacy controls once the advertising integration is enabled. You can also delete locally stored Plantoria data using the app's available controls or Android application-data controls.

10. Children

Plantoria is a general-audience plant-care application and is not designed specifically for children. The advertising configuration must follow the applicable Google policies for the intended audience before public release.

11. Security

Plantoria is designed to keep its core plant-care data local. Information transmitted to online services is sent using the transport security provided by those services and Android networking. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Plantoria features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
